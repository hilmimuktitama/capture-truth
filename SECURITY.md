# Security and privacy

Capture Truth processes supplied exports locally and performs no Jira or Confluence fetch. Candidate derivation metadata is enumerable and versioned so privacy decisions cannot be lost by serialization. Portable profiles structurally exclude candidate claims from `raw_body` and `mixed` material, plus legacy, invalid, or source-inconsistent claims. No caller-provided boolean or prose heuristic can override that policy. Portable outputs use an allowlist and redact common secret patterns across URL, path, locator, keys, metadata, fields, and caveats. Redaction is defense-in-depth, not a confidentiality guarantee. Review portable output before sharing. Raw-local-only output may contain exact confidential prose and must stay local. MCP normalize responses are raw-free even though the local normalize utility can retain raw for explicit local workflows.
